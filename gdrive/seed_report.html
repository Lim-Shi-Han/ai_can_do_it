Summary

Between 14:10 and 15:02 SGT, payment-service authorization requests timed out because the database connection pool was saturated. After reconciliation with the payment processor exports, the final count is 1,312 timed-out payment attempts. This replaces the provisional estimate of 1,240 published on the Confluence customer impact page.

  • Unique customers with at least one timed-out attempt: 871.
  • Automatic client retries included in the 1,312: 214.
  • Singapore: 1,004 timed-out attempts. Malaysia: 308.
  • No duplicate charges were found. Every timed-out attempt either failed cleanly or succeeded on retry.
